OUR STORY

WHO WE ARE.

Mususumeli Logistics is focused on the transportation of construction materials and equipment.

We recognise the important role transportation plays in construction and development. Materials and equipment need to reach their destinations to support the work taking place on site.

Our focus is on providing transport solutions that respond to construction-related requirements and support the movement of projects forward.
